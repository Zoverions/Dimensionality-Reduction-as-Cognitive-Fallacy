## Primary hypothesis protocol

Before confirmatory analysis, freeze a versioned protocol specifying:

- the primary hypothesis and falsification condition;
- survey/data release, sample construction, mass cuts, redshift range, and exclusions;
- graph construction, edge weighting, normalization, and parameter ranges;
- primary endpoint and multiple-comparison handling;
- null models and preregistered sensitivity analyses;
- software environment, seeds, checksums, commands, and expected artifacts.

Exploratory notebooks and parameter searches must be stored separately and labelled exploratory.

## Required controls

1. Randomized and degree/geometry-preserving null graphs as appropriate.
2. Survey-selection and completeness sensitivity.
3. Graph-definition, mass-cut, redshift, and sample-size sensitivity.
4. Probe invariance across declared dynamics rather than a single diffusion choice.
5. Synthetic recovery tests with known ground truth.
6. Negative/null findings retained with the successful outputs.
7. External methods review before stronger public claims.
